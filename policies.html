Policies and use guidelines

VitalConnect exists to support safe, respectful, and constructive engagement.

Community standards

Communicate respectfully and share only what is appropriate for a supportive community. Do not post abusive language, harassment, misleading claims, or another person's private information. Prayer requests and help requests often carry sensitive detail — treat what you read here as told in confidence.

Privacy notice

This project stores all data in your own browser using LocalStorage. Nothing is transmitted to a server, and no analytics or third-party scripts are loaded. Anyone with access to your device and browser profile can read that data, so treat a shared or public computer accordingly and sign out when you are finished.

Your data and your control

Your profile page can export everything associated with your account as a JSON file, and can delete your account together with every entry you have posted. Clearing your browser's site data removes it just as completely. Because there is no server copy, deleted data cannot be recovered.

Academic scope

VitalConnect is coursework built for academic review. Passwords are salted and stretched rather than stored in plaintext, but a browser-only application has no server-side secret and cannot offer production-grade account security. Do not reuse a password here that protects anything important.

Safety

VitalConnect is not a medical service, a counselling service, or an emergency service, and no one is monitoring posts for crises. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number. In the United States you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, twenty-four hours a day.
