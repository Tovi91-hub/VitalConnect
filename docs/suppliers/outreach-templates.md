# Supplier Outreach Templates

Fill every `[bracket]` before sending. Do not send a message with an unverified claim in
it — see "Positioning claim to verify" in [`supplier-strategy.md`](supplier-strategy.md).

Send Medline and McKesson the same week. Medline is the primary because the drop-ship
program is published rather than negotiated; McKesson runs in parallel because its
approval path is slower.

---

## 1. Medline — Dealer Drop Ship Program

Route: partner/account request form, then follow up by phone at 1-800-MEDLINE. The phone
call matters more than the form here; distributor account reps move applications that have
a name attached to them.

**Subject:** Dealer Drop Ship Program — account request, MyVital Harmony LLC

> Hello,
>
> I am [name], [title] of MyVital Harmony LLC, a [state]-registered online retailer of
> home-health and medical supplies operating at myvitalharmony.com.
>
> I am writing to request a dealer account with access to the Drop Ship Program. Our model
> is direct-to-consumer e-commerce with a curated catalog rather than a broad listing
> approach, concentrated in home monitoring, mobility and safety, incontinence and
> caregiver supplies, wound care, PPE, and orthopedic supports. We also expect a meaningful
> share of volume from small business accounts — home-care agencies and assisted-living
> operators buying on a recurring monthly basis.
>
> Our position today: [live and selling, monthly order volume X / launching in Q_, catalog
> built and awaiting supplier confirmation]. We hold [EIN, resale certificate, product
> liability coverage of $__ per occurrence] and can provide documentation immediately.
>
> Three questions so we can size the opportunity accurately:
>
> 1. Does the Drop Ship Program admit direct-to-consumer e-commerce retailers, or is it
>    limited to accredited HME providers? If accreditation is required, what is the
>    appropriate account type for a retailer in our position?
> 2. What product data is available to dealers — a catalog feed, API, or scheduled file —
>    including images, descriptions, inventory status, and dealer pricing?
> 3. Can you confirm the current drop-ship fee structure and the flat shipping rate, and
>    whether it applies per shipment or per line item?
>
> I am glad to complete a credit application and provide trade references. Who is the
> right person to work with on this?
>
> Thank you,
> [name] · [title] · MyVital Harmony LLC · [phone] · [email]

Phone-call version, kept to twenty seconds: *"I'm calling to open a dealer account for
drop shipping. We're an online home-health retailer, direct to consumer, and I want to
confirm whether your Drop Ship Program takes e-commerce retailers or whether it's limited
to accredited HME providers."* Then stop talking and let them route you.

## 2. McKesson Medical-Surgical — authorized e-commerce reseller

Route: e-commerce services enquiry. **Do not** open a standard SupplyManager account and
begin listing — their Terms of Sale restrict purchases to the buyer's own use and treat
resale to unauthorized parties as diversion.

**Subject:** Authorized e-commerce reseller enquiry — MyVital Harmony LLC

> Hello,
>
> I am [name] of MyVital Harmony LLC, an online medical-supply retailer. I am contacting
> the e-commerce services team specifically, rather than opening a standard purchasing
> account, because I understand your Terms of Sale limit purchases to the buyer's own use
> and require prior written consent for resale. We want the relationship structured
> correctly from the start.
>
> What we are seeking: an authorized e-commerce reseller and direct-to-consumer
> fulfillment relationship, in which McKesson picks, packs, and ships qualifying orders
> directly to our retail customers.
>
> About us: [state]-registered LLC selling home-health and medical supplies at
> myvitalharmony.com, focused on home monitoring, mobility and safety, caregiver supplies,
> wound care, and PPE, serving both consumers and small institutional accounts
> [home-care agencies, assisted living]. Current status: [live / launching in Q_], with
> [order volume or forecast]. We carry [product liability coverage] and hold [EIN, resale
> certificate, applicable state registrations].
>
> Specifically, I would like to understand:
>
> 1. What qualification criteria apply to an authorized e-commerce reseller, and what
>    written consent is required before any product is listed online?
> 2. Which product categories are eligible for direct-to-patient fulfillment, and which
>    are excluded?
> 3. What catalog and order-integration options exist — feed, API, EDI?
> 4. What restrictions apply to channel, pricing, and brand presentation?
>
> We are prepared to work within whatever authorization framework applies and will not
> list any McKesson-sourced product before written consent is in place.
>
> Thank you,
> [name] · [title] · MyVital Harmony LLC · [phone] · [email]

That last sentence is worth keeping. It is the sentence that separates this enquiry from
the ones their compliance team declines.

## 3. Meddcare LLC — wholesale / dropship program

Shorter and more direct; smaller suppliers respond to specifics, not positioning.

**Subject:** Wholesale + dropship account — online medical supply retailer

> Hello,
>
> I am [name] of MyVital Harmony LLC, an online medical-supply retailer at
> myvitalharmony.com. I would like to open a wholesale account with dropship fulfillment.
>
> Our launch catalog is 50–150 SKUs across home monitoring, mobility and safety, wound
> care, PPE, incontinence and caregiver supplies, and orthopedic supports.
>
> To evaluate fit, could you send:
>
> 1. Wholesale price list and MOQ for the categories above
> 2. Dropship fee, shipping rates, and typical time from order to ship
> 3. Product data feed or API details, including live inventory
> 4. Return and warranty terms, and your recall/lot-traceability process
> 5. Which brands you are an authorized distributor for
> 6. Whether you ship from your own U.S. warehouse
>
> I can provide our resale certificate, EIN, and insurance certificate on request.
>
> Thank you,
> [name] · MyVital Harmony LLC · [phone] · [email]

## What to have ready when they answer

An accepted application moves fast, and the questions that follow it are always the same:
projected monthly volume, categories, channel, whether you sell on marketplaces, who your
other suppliers are, and your credit references. Have the answers written down before the
first call so the numbers do not change between conversations.
