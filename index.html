What members can do

Track how you are doing

Log a mood in about fifteen seconds, add a note if you want to, and watch the trend build over days and weeks. Export it as a CSV whenever you like.

Ask for prayer

Post a request to the wall and see who is praying with you. Members can mark that they are praying, so support is visible rather than assumed.

Give and receive help

Offer items or services in the marketplace, or post a practical need on the help board and let neighbours volunteer.
